# Policies — Sunrise Family Clinic

## Cancellation Policy
- Appointments can be cancelled or rescheduled free of charge up to 4 hours before the scheduled time.
- Cancellations made less than 4 hours before the appointment, or no-shows, will be charged a ₹200 fee, payable at your next visit.

## Refund Policy
- Consultation fees are non-refundable once the consultation has taken place.
- If an appointment is cancelled by the clinic (e.g., doctor unavailability), the full amount paid will be refunded within 5-7 business days or you may choose to reschedule.
- Diagnostic test fees are non-refundable once the sample has been collected or the test has been performed.

## Working Hours
- Monday to Saturday: 9:00 AM - 7:00 PM
- Sunday: Closed
- Public holidays: Closed (emergency contact available, see About page)

## Payment Methods
- We accept cash, all major debit/credit cards, UPI, and select insurance providers.

## Privacy Policy
- All patient records and conversation data are kept confidential and used only for providing care, appointment management, and legally required reporting. Data is not shared with third parties without consent, except as required by law.
