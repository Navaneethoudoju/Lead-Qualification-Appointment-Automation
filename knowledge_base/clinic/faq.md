# Frequently Asked Questions — Sunrise Family Clinic

## What are your working hours?
We are open Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

## Do I need an appointment or can I walk in?
We recommend booking an appointment to avoid waiting time, but walk-ins are accepted based on availability.

## Where are you located?
Sunrise Family Clinic is located at 14 MG Road, near City Central Mall, Hyderabad, Telangana - 500001.

## Do you accept insurance?
We accept most major health insurance providers for outpatient consultations. Please bring your insurance card and ID for verification. For a specific insurer, please check with our front desk.

## Can I get a home visit?
Home visits are available for select services (general consultation, vaccination) within a 5 km radius, subject to doctor availability. An additional home visit fee of ₹300 applies.

## How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?
You can cancel or reschedule up to 4 hours before your appointment with no charge. Cancellations within 4 hours may incur a ₹200 fee. See our cancellation policy for full details.

## Do you treat children?
Yes, our general physicians and pediatric-trained staff see patients of all ages, including infants and children.

## What documents should I bring for my first visit?
Please bring a government-issued photo ID, any previous medical records or prescriptions, and your insurance card if applicable.

## Do you offer online consultations?
Yes, video consultations are available for follow-ups and non-emergency issues. Please mention this when booking.

## Is parking available?
Yes, free parking is available for patients in the basement of the building.
